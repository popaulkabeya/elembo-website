Who we are

A completely connected technology & supply partner

Based in Kitwe, Copperbelt Province, Elembo Business Technology Limited is the bridge between suppliers, manufacturers and clients. We simplify procurement, reduce costs and deliver dependable, end-to-end technical solutions for banks, hospitals, offices, mining, farms, industries and schools across Africa.
